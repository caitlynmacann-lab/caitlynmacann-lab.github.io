INFORMATION/HELP PAGE 🥬

Are you confused?

Hello there!

Since this website was created for a Level 1 Digital Technologies internal, rather than something professional, I am not particularly proficient in
coding a quiz website. Additionally, I don't have the time to learn lots of new skills in time for the internal. This means that I'm unable to do
many of the things that make a quiz website easy to use and simple for you.

I'm afraid that this means you'll have to do some things in order to prevent annoyance at my website!

  • Spell everything correctly: This is important for the biology external as well! If you misspell something you might lose a mark 😔
  • Capital letters: Most of the words you'll need to be entering are in the middle of a sentence, therefore they don't require capitals! However, if they're at the beginning of the sentence, they will need them.
  • Check for spaces: If you have a space in front or behind of the word you enter, it won't register the word as correct
  • Incorrect answer?: If you get it wrong, it will tell you the right answer. You can try again or just move onto the next question!
  • Cheat sheet: There are definitions prepared by Mrs Kumar here if you get stuck! There are all the definitions in the quiz and more :) Just a note, if you aren't part of SHCG you may not be able to view the document.
